The Sacred Rituals

A Telugu Brahmin wedding unfolds as a series of Vedic rituals, each one a blessing for the couple’s life together.

స్నాతకం

Snathakam

The groom completes his student life, takes the sacred thread and is blessed to enter married life.

శుభం

కాశీ యాత్ర

Kashi Yatra

In playful tradition the groom sets off for Kashi with umbrella and staff, until the bride’s brother persuades him to marry.

శుభం

ఎదురుకోలు

Edurukolu

The two families come face to face and welcome each other with music, flowers and joy.

శుభం

కన్యాదానం

Kanyadanam

The bride’s parents place her hand in the groom’s, praying for her lifelong happiness.

శుభం

జీలకర్ర బెల్లం

Jeelakarra Bellam

At the muhurtham the tera is lowered and the couple place cumin and jaggery on each other’s head — a bond both strong and sweet.

శుభం

మాంగల్య ధారణ

Mangalya Dharana

The groom ties the mangalasutram as “Mangalyam tantunanena” is chanted.

శుభం

తలంబ్రాలు

Talambralu

Bride and groom shower each other with turmeric rice and pearls, wishing each other abundance.

శుభం

సప్తపది

Saptapadi

Seven steps around the sacred fire, each one a vow for the life they share.

శుభం

అరుంధతి నక్షత్రం

Arundhati Nakshatram

The couple is shown the Arundhati star, the timeless symbol of a devoted, unwavering bond.
